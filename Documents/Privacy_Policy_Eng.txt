 Funtomin Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Funtomin has been developed as an educational and local multiplayer game and is designed to operate entirely on your device.

Funtomin does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The application does not require user registration, account creation, or the submission of personal data.

 Local Data Storage

Any game settings, scores, preferences, or other application data are stored locally on the user's device and are used solely to provide the intended functionality of the game.
